## Background

## Problem

## Reason for change
This chapter should serve as the canonical source for state/persistence boundaries only. Full field lists and CRUD/DB-operation enumerations increase staleness risk without adding decision value.

## Implementation intent
Keep this chapter as the canonical source for state/persistence boundaries. Remove full field lists and CRUD/DB-operation enumerations. Preserve scope/ownership boundaries between session/turn/persistent state and between databases.
