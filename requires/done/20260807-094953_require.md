## Background
The `05_agent_*.md` document set mixes design/operational judgment with content that is mechanically derivable from code. A review (`memo-doc-agent-review.md`) identified what to keep vs. remove per chapter.

## Problem
`docs/05_agent_01_system-overview.md` contains detailed entry-point call sequences, mechanical class/file correspondence tables, prompt literal strings, and exhaustive slash-command enumerations that will drift from source and add no decision value.

## Reason for change
This chapter should serve as the canonical source for overall scope and top-level execution model only. Component-level detail belongs in the chapters that own it. Mechanical inventories increase staleness risk without adding decision value.

## Implementation intent
Keep this chapter as the canonical source for overall scope and the top-level execution model. Push component-level detail to the chapters that own it. Remove detailed entry-point call sequences, mechanical class/file correspondence tables, prompt literal strings, and exhaustive slash-command enumerations. Replace overlap with pointers to owning chapters.
