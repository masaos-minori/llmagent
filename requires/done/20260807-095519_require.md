## Background
The `05_agent_*.md` document set mixes design/operational judgment with content that is mechanically derivable from code. A review (`memo-doc-agent-review.md`) identified what to keep vs. remove per chapter.

## Problem
The tool-execution-and-approval chapter covers approval, fail-closed behavior, DAG scheduling, and side-effect serialization — all safety-relevant — but also carries `ToolCallResult` field lists, detailed internal call order inside `execute()`, duplicated approve/reject argument explanations, full GitHub-tool-name enumeration, preview-format tables, full audit-log field enumeration, and method/helper-function detail that are better left to code.

## Reason for change
This chapter should serve as the canonical source for tool-execution/approval/safety design intent only. Field lists and call-order detail increase staleness risk without adding decision value. However, safety-relevant judgment must never be dropped even while trimming code-level detail.

## Implementation intent
Keep this chapter as the canonical source for tool-execution/approval/safety design. Remove field lists and call-order detail, but never at the expense of safety rationale. Re-verify after trimming: every safety-relevant judgment (approval, side effects, DAG, caching) still states its reasoning and operational caveat.
