## Background
`requires/done/20260813-172533_require.md` splits the `resolve_retryable` cleanup into two independently schedulable sub-tasks and explicitly gates sub-task 2 (extracting one shared three-branch classification helper used by both `LlmTransportErrorHandler` and `LlmReconnectHandler`, in `scripts/shared/llm_transport_errors.py` and `scripts/shared/llm_reconnect.py`) behind "explicit sign-off before implementation." The requirement does not say who grants that sign-off, through what channel (a PR review approval, a comment on the requirement/plan, a separate maintainer message), or what form of record should be left so a future implementer can point to it when starting sub-task 2.

No repo convention was found (`rules/coding.md`, `rules/toolchain.md`, `skills/require-to-plan/SKILL.md`, `skills/python-refactoring/SKILL.md`) for how a "requires explicit sign-off before implementation" gate is normally satisfied or recorded in this project.

## Problem
The sign-off mechanism for sub-task 2 (cross-file consolidation of `resolve_retryable` into a shared three-branch helper) is undefined. Without a clear channel and record format, a future implementer cannot determine when the gate is cleared.

## Reason for change
To ensure the high-risk refactoring (sub-task 2) proceeds only after proper authorization, while preventing ambiguity about when authorization has been granted.

## Implementation intent

Record whichever channel is used here or in the follow-up plan so the precedent is reusable for future two-sub-task requirements with a risk-gated second half.

Recommended approach: use a comment on `plans/20260813-190710_plan.md` since it is the most discoverable location for future implementers working on this plan.
