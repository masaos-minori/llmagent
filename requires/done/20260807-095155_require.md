## Background
The `05_agent_*.md` document set mixes design/operational judgment with content that is mechanically derivable from code. A review (`memo-doc-agent-review.md`) identified what to keep vs. remove per chapter.

## Problem
The turn-processing-flow chapter carries step-by-step function-call sequences, private method names, and dataclass field lists that are better left to code, while obscuring the core operational and audit-relevant concepts (correlation-ID, approval-wait, partial-completion, workflow-state).

## Reason for change
This chapter should serve as the canonical source for turn-processing design intent only. Sequential call-detail and dataclass field dumps increase staleness risk without adding decision value.

## Implementation intent
Keep this chapter as the canonical source for turn-processing design intent. Preserve correlation-ID / approval-wait / partial-completion / workflow-state concepts as operational and audit-relevant, described by intent rather than field enumeration. Remove sequential call-detail and dataclass field dumps.

## Implementation instructions
1. Read all four files in full.
2. Identify sections containing: sequential function-call walkthroughs, private method names, dataclass field lists, guard public-method enumerations, verbatim constant-string quotes, mechanical state-transition tables (e.g., `current_turn_id`).

Remove or compress those sections while preserving: conceptual model of a single turn, why WorkflowEngine is mandatory, meaning of plan/execute/verify, operational notes for approval-wait/background-failure/pause states, ToolLoopGuard's role and design intent, reason partial completions are separated from conversation history.
