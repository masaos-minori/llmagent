## Background

## Reason for change
The document violates the design-doc reduction policy established in `memo-doc-eventbus-review.md`. Detail should be redirected to the source files rather than deleted, ensuring no loss of information for readers who need it.

## Implementation intent
Keep this chapter focused on why HTTP status (not JSON body) should be the primary monitoring signal, what `ok`/`degraded` mean, and representative degraded causes to check. Redirect readers to `health_route.py` for exact response format.
