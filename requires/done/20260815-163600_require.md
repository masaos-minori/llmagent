# AgentContext thread safety: ConversationState.history lacks synchronization

## Priority
Low

## Summary
ConversationState.history has no synchronization mechanism. While asyncio is single-threaded, the absence of explicit locking makes the design assumption implicit rather than documented.

## Reason for Change
TurnState.tool_calls is protected by asyncio.Lock (_lock), but ConversationState.history is accessed without synchronization. The design relies on asyncio being single-threaded, but this assumption is not explicitly documented.
