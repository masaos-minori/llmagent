# Remove docs/00_index.md body sections that duplicate its own frontmatter

## Priority
Low

## Target files
- `docs/00_index.md` (frontmatter `tags` lines 4-9, `related` lines 10-17; body "Related
  Documents" section ~lines 151-159; body "Keywords" section ~lines 161-168)

## Background
`docs/00_index.md` carries frontmatter fields `tags` (5 entries) and `related` (7
entries) that are the machine-readable metadata for this document. The document body
also contains a "## Related Documents" section and a "## Keywords" section that repeat
the same information as plain Markdown lists for human readers.

This was verified directly against the current file content: the frontmatter `related`
list (lines 10-17: `01_overview.md`, `02_deployment-part1.md`,
`03_rag_00_document-guide.md`, `04_mcp_00_document-guide.md`,
`05_agent_00_document-guide.md`, `06_eventbus_00_document-guide.md`,
`90_shared_00_document-guide.md`) exactly matches the body "Related Documents" list
(lines 151-159), item for item and in the same order (differing only in backtick
formatting, not content). The frontmatter `tags` list (lines 4-9: `documentation`,
`navigation`, `overview`, `index`, `knowledge-base`) exactly matches the body "Keywords"
list (lines 161-168), item for item and in the same order.

## Problem
The same two lists are maintained in two places in the same file: once in frontmatter,
once in the body. Any future addition, removal, or reordering of a related document or
a keyword must be applied twice to stay consistent, and nothing enforces that the two
copies stay in sync — they can silently diverge over time.

## Reason for change
Removing the duplicated body sections leaves frontmatter as the single source of truth
for these two lists, eliminating the dual-maintenance burden and the drift risk, with no
loss of information (frontmatter retains both lists in full).

## Implementation intent
Keep `docs/00_index.md` frontmatter (`tags` and `related`) unchanged and treat it as the
sole record of the keywords and related-documents information. Delete the redundant
body-level "## Related Documents" and "## Keywords" sections entirely, since their
content is already present in frontmatter.

## Implementation instructions
1. Immediately before editing, re-diff the current body "Related Documents" list against
   the current frontmatter `related` field, and the current body "Keywords" list against
   the current frontmatter `tags` field, to reconfirm both still match exactly (line
   numbers may have shifted since this requirement was written).
2. If either pair of lists differs in any entry (missing item, extra item, different
   wording, different order), stop and report the discrepancy instead of deleting
   anything — do not silently reconcile or delete in that case.
3. If both pairs match exactly, remove the entire "## Related Documents" section
   (heading plus its bullet list) and the entire "## Keywords" section (heading plus its
   list) from the body of `docs/00_index.md`.
4. Do not modify the frontmatter (`tags`, `related`, or any other frontmatter field) or
   its formatting.
5. Do not modify any other section of `docs/00_index.md` (e.g. `## カテゴリ`, `##
   推奨読書順序`, `## 既知の問題`, `## タスク別ドキュメント参照`).
6. This is a documentation-only change with no code or behavior impact.

## Acceptance criteria
- `docs/00_index.md` no longer contains a body-level "## Related Documents" section or a
  body-level "## Keywords" section.
- `docs/00_index.md` frontmatter `tags` and `related` fields are unchanged in content,
  order, and formatting.
- No other section of `docs/00_index.md` is altered.
- No information is lost: every item previously listed in the body sections remains
  present in frontmatter.

## Tests
Not applicable in the automated-test sense (documentation-only, no behavior change).
Verification is manual: after the change, confirm via `grep -n "^## Related Documents\|^## Keywords" docs/00_index.md`
that neither heading remains, and confirm via a frontmatter read that `tags` and
`related` are unchanged from before the edit.
