## Problem
- Rationale 3 says keeping `content` as a separate field "prevents the original text from being lost to normalization" (Explicit in ADR-009 line 105) and Positive Consequences says "Data loss on normalization failure is prevented" (Explicit in line 190), while the design drops all chunks of a file whose normalization fails (Explicit in ADR-009 item 6, INV-05 and the Fail-Fast condition) and RAG-002 drops a sentence together with its original text (Explicit in governance_03 RAG-002).
- No invariant is violated by RAG-002: INV-01 to INV-10 do not state what happens to a sentence whose normalized text is empty (Explicit in the Invariants section).
- The Known Deviations entry names RAG-002 without the invariant it violates (Explicit in line 328).
- The Completion Checklist says the Known Issue "is not yet registered in `governance_03_issue-and-uncertainty-management.md`" although RAG-002 is registered (Confirmed by repository evidence — ledger row `RAG-002`).
- The Completion Checklist and Manual Review list INV-05, INV-06, INV-08 and INV-09 as having no automated test; INV-11 will have none until the regression test of the open plan exists.

## Reason for change
- A reader trusting Rationale 3 would believe normalization can never cost data, which the file-level skip and RAG-002 contradict; the invariant makes the intended behavior checkable.
