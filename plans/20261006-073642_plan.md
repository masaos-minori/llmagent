**Architecture (lightweight).** The three changed tools (`check_docs_structure.py`, `manage_frontmatter.py`, `check_docs_quality.py`) live under `tools/` as standalone CLI entrypoints; they do not import or feed the production runtime under `scripts/`, and no `scripts/` module imports them. Their only dependents are CI, `.pre-commit-config.yaml`, and `tests/tools/`. There is no DB schema or MCP-server lock involved. This low coupling justifies the scope: changes are isolated to the docs-tooling surface and its test/CI consumers.

**Dependency graph.** `check_docs_structure.py` is invoked by CI (blocking step) and `routing.md`; `manage_frontmatter.py merge-related` by authors/tests; `check_docs_quality.py` by pre-commit. Strengthening `check_docs_structure.py` therefore requires updating its test suite, its TOOL_DESCRIPTIONS.md/routing.md descriptions, and the CI invocation together — hence the "coordinate as one change" intent.

**Historical analysis.** `check_docs_structure.py` has 13 commits, `manage_frontmatter.py` and `check_docs_quality.py` 7 each — moderate churn, no sign of active parallel work on these lines. No merge conflict risk expected.

**Operational dependency inspection.** N/A — no production runtime, DB, or MCP server is touched; no deploy step is required (all targets are docs/tooling/CI, none under `/opt/llm/`).
