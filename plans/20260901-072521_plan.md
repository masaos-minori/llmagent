## Background
Discovered while updating ADR-001 to Accepted status (2026-08-31) and cross-checking its own embedded Known Deviations (which already tracked and resolved a WF-001/WF-002/WF-003 set distinct from, but same-named as, the entries in `05_agent_90_inconsistencies_and_known_issues.md`). ADR-001's own Known Deviations WF-001 described a citation-format issue between two line-numbered ADR-001 references and was resolved by a docstring correction (confirmed current INV-01/INV-05 text is already distinct); it did not describe the same invariant content that `05_agent_90`'s WF-001 attributes to INV-01/INV-05.

Key evidence:
- ADR-001 Decision Details are numbered 1-9 (not INV-01 through INV-05). There are NO separate INV-01 or INV-05 entries in ADR-001.
- WF-001's Summary claims INV-01 ("All execution paths must flow through the Workflow Engine") and INV-05 ("The Workflow Engine is the sole orchestrator of tool execution") express the same invariant. Neither text appears in ADR-001.
- ADR-001 Decision Detail #1 states: "WorkflowEngineは必須である。ワークフロー定義ファイルはデプロイ必須成果物である。欠落または検証失敗時はAgent起動を中止する。"
- ADR-001 Decision Detail #2 states: "Agentによる外部状態変更、Tool実行、複数ステップ処理、承認を必要とする操作はすべてWorkflow Engineの管理下で実行する。"
- ADR-001 Decision Detail #5 states: "すべてのAgent処理は、単純な質問応答を含め、Workflow Engineの管理下に置かれる。処理が単純であることは、Workflow Engineを迂回する理由にはならない。"
- ADR-001 Decision Detail #7 states: "実行成功と検証成功を区別する。"

WF-002 claims INV-03 exists and states "When the Workflow Engine reports successful execution, the corresponding document state must reflect that execution." No such INV-03 exists in ADR-001.

WF-003 claims Decision #5 specifies a simple Q&A single-stage workflow that is not implemented. Decision #5 actually says all Agent processing including simple Q&A should be under Workflow Engine management — it does NOT specify a separate "Q&A single-stage workflow" implementation.

## Problem
`05_agent_90_inconsistencies_and_known_issues.md`'s WF-001 entry's "Summary" and "Current Description" fields quote INV-01/INV-05 wording that does not appear anywhere in the current `docs/adr/ADR-001-workflow-engine-mandatory.md`. It is unclear whether this entry describes an earlier draft of ADR-001 that was later rewritten, a transcription error, or a conflation with a different document.

Similarly, WF-002 and WF-003 reference non-existent INV-03 and Decision #5 misinterpretation respectively.

## Reason for change
A canonical Known Issues document (per this repository's Document Classification, Known Issues documents are meant to track confirmed discrepancies against current documents) that quotes invariant text not present in the ADR it cites undermines trust in the rest of its entries and could send an investigator looking for a mismatch that does not exist as described.
