## Reason for change

design goal `documentation-checks.md` itself already partially models (its
line-240 "Canonical source" note pointing to `documentation-policy.md`
instead of repeating content) — extended here to the 3 sections that don't
yet follow that pattern.

## Implementation intent
For each of the 3 rule clusters, replace `documentation-checks.md`'s full
restatement with a short summary (stating only what the check verifies) and
a link to the corresponding section in `documentation-policy.md`. For the
Merge Conditions cluster specifically, first relocate the `GV-020`-related
non-blocking-condition bullet into `documentation-policy.md`'s "##  Merge
Conditions" > Non-Blocking Conditions list, so the canonical copy gains the
one piece of content that was previously only in the non-canonical copy,
before removing that content from `documentation-checks.md`. Keep every
target heading in `documentation-checks.md` in place (only body content
changes) — no anchor-breakage risk exists (see Background), but there is
also no independent reason to remove the headings, since they still serve
as the numbered-check-list entry points readers navigate to.
