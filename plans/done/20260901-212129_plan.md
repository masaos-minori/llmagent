## Background

The actual mechanism lives in Step 1: "**All-steps-completed check**: after reading the file, inspect its `## Execution Status` table. If every step row shows `Completed`... the procedure is fully executed — do not re-execute it. Move it to `implementations/done/`..." This is a stronger, more directly stated idempotency mechanism than either upstream phase has (see `ptip004`'s finding that `plan-to-implementation-procedure` lacks an equivalent short-circuit entirely) — but it is not referenced from the section whose name most directly suggests this topic.
