# Plan: Automate glossary term classification validation (GV-015)

## Goal
Add a new checker under `tools/` that parses the "Compatibility and Lifecycle Terminology"
glossary table in `docs/00_governance/governance_02_documentation-metadata.md` and flags
entries whose term is a general/common English word rather than a project-specific
identifier, then mark GV-015 `Existing`. Keep the check non-blocking and `Warning`-level.

Low

  - Update the GV-015 matrix row to `Existing` and clear its Follow-up note.
- **Out-of-Scope**:

## Background
`governance_04_documentation-checks.md` maps GV-015 ("Glossary limited to project-specific
terms") to human review on a periodic basis, status `Partial`, with the follow-up
"Automate glossary term classification validation (currently manual)". The glossary lives in
the "Terminology Glossary" section of `governance_02_documentation-metadata.md`, whose body
states "The glossary holds project-specific terms only" and defines usage rules (CamelCase /
proper nouns capitalized; uppercase abbreviations; general English words excluded except for
American-spelling conventions such as `Authorization`, `Behavior`, `Standardization`).

## Problem
New glossary entries may introduce general English words used in their generic sense,
violating the "project-specific terms only" rule. Manual review only catches this between
sweeps.

## Reason for change
GV-015 is a periodic `Warning` gate. Automating classification keeps the glossary disciplined
and removes reliance on manual sweeps.

## Implementation intent
Build a new checker that parses the glossary term table in `governance_02_documentation-metadata.md`
and flags entries whose term is a general/common English word rather than a project-specific
identifier. Classification is inherently heuristic; start from conservative signals (casing
conventions matching the usage rules, plus a curated blocklist of common words) and refine over
time. Keep the check non-blocking.

## Requirements
- `REQ-001`: The checker parses the glossary term table from
  `governance_02_documentation-metadata.md`.
- `REQ-002`: Each entry is classified using conservative signals derived from

- `REQ-003`: An entry that is a general/common English word (lowercase-starting or matching a
  curated common-word blocklist, and not on the documented allowlist) is flagged at `Warning`.
- `REQ-004`: No false positives on the current canonical term set (the seven existing entries).
- `REQ-005`: The GV-015 matrix row status changes from `Partial` to `Existing` and its
  Follow-up note is cleared.

## Design
New standalone CLI following the `tools/check_*.py` convention (argparse, `Issue` results,
`Warning`/`ERROR` severities).

1. **Parse the glossary.** Locate the "Compatibility and Lifecycle Terminology" table under the
   "Terminology Glossary" section of `governance_02` and extract the `Term` column cells.
2. **Classify (UNK-01).** For each term:
   - CamelCase / multi-word PascalCase, or an all-uppercase abbreviation → project-specific →
     pass.
   - Lowercase-starting (not capitalized) → casing-convention violation → treat as a
     general/common word → flag at `Warning`.
   - Capitalized single word → consult the curated common-word blocklist; if it matches and is
     not on the documented allowlist, flag; otherwise pass.
3. **Allowlist.** Never flag the documented American-spelling exceptions
   (`Authorization`, `Behavior`, `Standardization`).
4. **Reporting.** `Warning`-only; preserve the non-blocking contract.

Scope boundary (UNK-02): the classifier catches lowercase-starting general words and
blocklisted common words; it does not attempt to flag capitalized general words, which would
false-positive on the legitimate capitalized terms `Default`, `Migration`, `Obsolete`.

No change to `governance_02` glossary content, definitions, or usage rules.

## Risks
- **Risk**: Heuristic classification may false-positive on legitimate project terms. →
  **Mitigation**: Conservative casing-primary signal; documented allowlist; fail safe toward no
  false positive (UNK-01, UNK-03).
- **Risk**: Cannot fully classify capitalized general words without false-positives on
  `Default`/`Migration`/`Obsolete`. → **Mitigation**: Document this as a scope boundary; the
  classifier targets the catchable failure mode (UNK-02).
- **Risk**: A curated common-word blocklist may be incomplete or drift. → **Mitigation**:
  Start small, refine over time, and keep it excluding legitimate/documented terms (UNK-03).
