## Design Decision: merge vs. keep-separate-and-cross-reference

- **Decision: keep the two entries separate, cross-referenced.** Do not
  merge "shared common config" (under "Deprecated Concepts") into
  `common.toml` (under "Deprecated Configuration Files") as a single entry.
- Rationale:
  - The two entries live under different taxonomy sections of the document
    ("Deprecated Concepts" vs. "Deprecated Configuration Files") that this
    document's own structure treats as distinct categories (a named
    architectural idea vs. a specific file). Merging would require removing
    one section's bullet and changing the document's category structure,
    which is out of scope per `01_require-to-plan.md` ("do not change
    workflow directory structure" / no broad restructuring in a
    documentation-governance fix).
  - The source requirement's own Implementation Intent and Instructions
    (`requires/20260727-141020_require.md`, steps 2-5) describe updating the
    "shared common config" entry's four fields and adding a cross-reference
    to the `common.toml` entry — they do not instruct deleting or merging
    either bullet.
  - The Maintenance Rule in `docs/00_governance_05_deprecated-items.md`
    ("Items cannot be removed without documenting what replaced them") makes
    outright removal/merging of one bullet a heavier, riskier edit than a
    same-shape field update with a cross-reference; keeping both entries
    with a clear cross-reference achieves the requirement's goal (no stale
    duplicate open question) without that added risk.
