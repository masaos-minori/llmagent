## Goal
- Make front matter `related:` the single source of truth for cross-references under `docs/`: merge each document's body `## Related Documents` content into it, remove the body section from non-ADR documents, keep ADR body sections, and align the tooling and governance text with that model.

## Priority
Medium

## Scope
- **In-Scope**:
  - A `merge-related` subcommand in `tools/manage_frontmatter.py` (dry-run default) with tests.
  - `tools/rename_doc.py` rewriting front matter `related:` entries on rename, with tests.
  - `tools/check_docs_structure.py` rule changes (Related Documents required for ADR only; leftover body Related sections flagged elsewhere; ADR front matter must cover body references), with tests.
  - Merging and removing body Related sections in 176 non-ADR documents and extending front matter in 13 ADR documents.
  - Refreshing the `EXPECTED_WITHIN_FILE_PAIRS` baseline in `tests/tools/test_check_docs_quality.py`.
  - Updating `tools/TOOL_DESCRIPTIONS.md`, `routing.md`, `prompts/08_document-sync.md`, and the governance metadata and documentation-checks documents.
- **Out-of-Scope**:
  - Changing the owner ruling that front matter is authoritative, the `source` field, or any other front matter field.
  - Restructuring ADR body sections or their sub-headings.
  - ADR documents whose front matter already covers their body references (no modification needed).
  - Any change under `scripts/`, `config/`, `schemas/`, or `deploy/`.
  - Ongoing auto-generation of front matter from body content.

## Background
- The owner ruled the divergence between the two fields to be drift, with front matter authoritative (`plans/done/20260927-121543_plan.md`).
- A follow-up cycle reconciled body lists to front matter while keeping both fields (`plans/done/20260927-170338_plan.md`; 64 procedures archived under `implementations/done/`). The two-field model stayed, so the duplication that causes drift remained.
- This Plan changes the model itself; it is not a duplicate of that cycle.

## Problem
Measured on 2026-10-04 over 194 `docs/**/*.md` files (basename comparison):
- 143 files have equal front matter and body lists; 51 differ.
- 50 non-ADR files carry more than one Related-type section: 46 files carry a `## Related Docs` or `## Related Chapters` section in addition to `## Related Documents`. This corrects the Issue's statement that these headings replace the required one.
- 34 non-ADR files have non-link lines inside their body Related section (for example descriptive text), so a mechanical deletion would lose information.
- 13 of 18 ADR documents reference documents in their body that their front matter omits.
- `tools/rename_doc.py` rewrites Markdown-link paths only, so a rename leaves stale front matter `related:` entries (detected by `check_related_links`, not repaired).
- `tests/tools/test_check_docs_quality.py` asserts an exact baseline that includes 'Related Documents' similarity pairs, so removing the sections breaks it until the baseline is refreshed.

## Reason for change
The ruling already makes front matter authoritative, so the body list carries no independent information in general documents and only creates drift. The last reconciliation did not converge. Removing the duplicate is the only permanent fix, and checkers then validate one field.

## Implementation intent
- Extend the existing front matter tool rather than adding a new one: it already treats `related` as a list field and already uses the dry-run-default plus `--fix` pattern.
- Review the full dry-run output with the owner before any document is written, then apply per documentation area in separately revertable commits.
- Keep ADR body sections (their sub-headings carry classification); make ADR front matter the flat superset.
- Change the checker only after documents are migrated, so each step leaves the repository passing its checks.
- Normalize front matter entries to basenames; basenames are unique by construction.

## Requirements
- `REQ-001`: `tools/manage_frontmatter.py` provides a `merge-related` subcommand, dry-run by default, that unions entries from `## Related Documents`, `## Related Docs`, and `## Related Chapters` (backtick and Markdown link forms, multiple sections) with front matter `related:`, drops self-references and duplicates, normalizes to basenames, keeps existing order, and reports additions, unresolved targets, and non-link lines per file. It scans `docs/**/*.md` recursively (the existing subcommands only glob top-level `docs/*.md`, a pre-existing limitation that is not fixed here) and rewrites only the `related:` block, handling a block list, an empty `related:`, and an inline `related: []`.
- `REQ-002`: `tools/rename_doc.py` rewrites front matter `related:` entries (bare filename and `../` styles) on rename and fails closed on any other shape. A bare entry matches by basename, the entry's existing style is preserved, and entries it rewrites are excluded from the non-link prose-mention report (today a bare front matter entry is reported there).
- `REQ-003`: `tools/check_docs_structure.py` requires `## Related Documents` for ADR documents only, flags Related-type body sections in non-ADR documents, and verifies each ADR's front matter `related:` covers the documents referenced in its body Related block.
- `REQ-004`: In every non-ADR document, body Related entries are merged into front matter `related:` and the body Related section(s) are removed; non-link lines are resolved by owner review, never silently dropped.
- `REQ-005`: In each ADR document whose front matter omits body-referenced documents, front matter `related:` is extended to cover them; ADR bodies are unchanged.
- `REQ-006`: `tools/TOOL_DESCRIPTIONS.md`, `routing.md`, `prompts/08_document-sync.md`, and the governance metadata and documentation-checks documents describe the new model, the ADR exception, and the tool changes.
- `REQ-007`: The `EXPECTED_WITHIN_FILE_PAIRS` baseline in `tests/tools/test_check_docs_quality.py` is regenerated so the exact-equality test passes after the migration.
