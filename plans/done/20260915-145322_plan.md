## Background
This Plan follows a review that classified every ADR's "Implementation Notes"
section into four buckets (delete, promote to ADR body, move to Known Issue, move to
Needs Confirmation). While auditing all 13 ADRs, ADR-004 was found to have a
structural deviation from the project's ADR template itself, filed as a standalone
issue rather than silently worked around.

## Problem
`docs/adr/ADR-004-environment-failure-handling-policy.md` has no `## Known Deviations`
heading (confirmed via `grep -n "^## " docs/adr/ADR-004-environment-failure-handling-policy.md`).
Instead, four Known Issue-shaped entries live under a non-standard heading,
`## Alignment with INV-01/INV-02`, which itself appears twice (lines 353 and 461) with
byte-identical content (confirmed by direct read of both occurrences). Two of the four
entries are marked "解消済み" (resolved, with plan-reference evidence); two are marked
"報告のみ（Known Issue未登録）" (report-only, not registered as a Known Issue) — one of
which the ADR's own text recommends registering as a new Known Issue but never does.

## Reason for change
A missing standard section heading means ADR-004's known deviations are not
discoverable the way every other ADR's are (a future tool that greps
`## Known Deviations` across `docs/adr/*.md` would silently miss ADR-004's four
entries). One of the two unregistered items is explicitly flagged by the ADR's own
text as needing registration but was never followed up.

## Implementation intent
Add a template-standard `## Known Deviations` heading to ADR-004, move and reformat
the four existing entries into it using ADR-002's `### {ID}: {title}` structured field
format (per this issue's explicit instruction, not the simpler bullet style some other
ADRs' `## Known Deviations` sections use), remove the duplicate
`## Alignment with INV-01/INV-02` occurrence, and close the governance-registration gap
for the one item that genuinely needs it.
