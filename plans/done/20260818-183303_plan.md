## 8. Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk**: Consolidating sections could break existing navigation patterns (Likelihood: medium — readers may rely on current document structure) → **Mitigation**: update TOCs and internal links before removing duplicated content.
- **Risk**: Near-duplicate content may have diverged (different versions in different docs) — merging could lose important context (Likelihood: low — diffs will reveal divergence) → **Mitigation**: when merging near-duplicates, include both versions' content in the canonical location with attribution notes.
- **Risk**: Over-consolidation could make individual documents too sparse (Likelihood: low — document guides should remain self-contained) → **Mitigation**: preserve essential context in each document even when referencing external sources.
