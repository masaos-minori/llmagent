# Plan: Missing Approval Record Across 10 Accepted ADRs

Generated at: 2026-09-01T22:33:51Z

## Goal
- Resolve the pervasive gap where 10 of 12 Accepted ADRs lack recorded approval evidence, violating the repository's own governance Merge Conditions (RACI approval requirement).

## Priority
Medium

## Scope
- **In-Scope**: 
  - Option (a): Collect real approval records for each of the 10 affected ADRs from the appropriate reviewer
  - Option (b): Amend governance policy (`docs/00_governance_01_documentation-policy.md`) to define an alternative acceptance-evidence standard
- **Out-of-Scope**: 
  - Re-evaluating whether any of the 10 ADRs' underlying architectural decisions are correct
  - ADR-003 (already has a real Approval Record) and deleted ADR-011/ADR-013

## Background
Surfaced while updating ADR-001 to Accepted status (2026-08-31). ADR-001's Approval Record was deliberately left as `pending` per explicit instruction not to fabricate an approver or date. Checking the rest of the ADR set found the same gap is pervasive, not specific to ADR-001.

## Problem
Ten of twelve current ADR files — including several already marked `Accepted` — have an Approval Record with `Approved By: pending` / `Approval Date: pending`, meaning no actual approval has been recorded for them. Only `ADR-003` currently has a real Approval Record (`Approved By: architecture-reviewer`, `Approval Date: 2026-08-20`).

Affected ADRs: ADR-001, ADR-002, ADR-004, ADR-005, ADR-006, ADR-007, ADR-008, ADR-009, ADR-010, ADR-012.

## Reason for Change
An `Accepted` ADR with no recorded approver or approval date does not satisfy this repository's own governance Merge Conditions (RACI approval), and using "pending" as if it were evidence of approval was explicitly called out as prohibited during the ADR-001 update. Leaving this unresolved across ten ADRs means any of them could be challenged as not actually meeting the Accepted bar this repository's own policy defines.

## Implementation Intent
This plan does not resolve the approvals itself — that requires an actual accountable-party decision per ADR, which only the architecture owner/RACI process can provide. The plan exists to make the gap visible and trackable: either (a) obtain and record real Approved By/Date/Reference values for each of the ten ADRs from the appropriate reviewer, or (b) if this repository's practical convention is that Status=Accepted by itself constitutes sufficient sign-off (bypassing a formal Approval Record), make that explicit as a governance policy amendment rather than leaving contradictory "Accepted + pending approval" text scattered across ten files.

## Requirements
- `REQ-001`: Every currently `Accepted` ADR must either have a real, non-"pending" Approval Record or governance policy must be amended to define an alternative acceptance-evidence standard
- `REQ-002`: No ADR may be left with contradictory "Accepted" status and an explicitly-flagged missing-approval note without a resolution path recorded
- `REQ-003`: No fabricated approver name, date, or reference may be added to close this issue faster
- `REQ-004`: No ADR may be silently changed back to `Proposed` as a workaround

## Design
Two mutually exclusive resolution paths:

**Option (a): Collect real approval records per ADR**
- Each of the 10 ADRs receives a real Approval Record with `Approved By`, `Approval Date`, and `Approval Reference` values filled in by the appropriate reviewer
- This requires obtaining actual approvals from the architecture owner/RACI process for each ADR
- Implementation is incremental: one ADR at a time, as each is actually reviewed and approved

**Option (b): Amend governance policy**
- Update `docs/00_governance_01_documentation-policy.md`'s ADR Status Definitions and/or Merge Conditions to state the actual current practice
- Remove or reword the Approval Record template's implication that a named approver/date is required
- This makes the policy consistent with the practical convention that Status=Accepted by itself constitutes sufficient sign-off
