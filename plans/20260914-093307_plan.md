## Background
`docs/03_rag_03_05_query_pipeline-augment-stages.md` section 5.5 (line 54) states only: "**Content-only Invariance Rule:** AugmentStage only formats `content` and never uses `normalized_content`. See ADR-009 for rationale, alternatives, and tradeoffs." `docs/adr/ADR-009-rag-ft5-text-separation.md` (Status: Accepted) already documents, in full: Decision Details item 11 (line 71) states the rule itself; item 3 (line 63) states FTS5 indexes `COALESCE(normalized_content, content)`; the Rationale section (lines 89-103) gives three named reasons (Search Quality, LLM Usability, Data Integrity); and the Scope section (lines 73-79) lists `AugmentStage` alongside `ChunkJapaneseMixin`, `RagRepository`, and `RagMaintenanceService` as in-scope components for the ADR as a whole (the "Content-only" rule itself, per Decision Details item 11, binds `AugmentStage`'s output specifically).

## Problem
A developer reading section 5.5 sees the rule stated but must follow the ADR-009 link and read the full document to understand what it means, why it exists, and its output-side tradeoff — exactly the gap the Issue's Evidence identifies.

## Reason for change
Per the Issue's Impact: developers unfamiliar with ADR-009 won't understand why `normalized_content` is excluded from output, and future changes to `AugmentStage` could accidentally reintroduce it. An inline summary, grounded in ADR-009's own text, reduces this risk without requiring a reader to context-switch to the full ADR for the basic facts.

## Implementation intent
Expand section 5.5's existing one-line "Content-only Invariance Rule" statement (line 54) into a short inline explanation covering: (1) what it means — `AugmentStage` always formats `content` (the readable original text) and never `normalized_content` (the search-only normalized text), per Decision Details item 11; (2) why it exists — FTS5 indexes `COALESCE(normalized_content, content)` for search (item 3), while an LLM needs the original readable text, not the normalized form, per Rationale §2 "LLM Usability"; (3) scope — the rule itself binds `AugmentStage`'s output specifically (item 11), while ADR-009 as a whole also governs `ChunkJapaneseMixin`/`RagRepository`/`RagMaintenanceService`'s handling of the two fields (Scope, lines 75-79) — the inline explanation must not overstate the rule's own scope beyond `AugmentStage`'s output; (4) the output-side tradeoff — Japanese content's Sudachi-normalized form (used for search quality) is never what reaches the LLM context; the original text is used instead, by design, per Rationale §1 vs §2's explicit Search-Quality-vs-LLM-Usability tradeoff — this is a documented design tradeoff, not an unaddressed limitation.
