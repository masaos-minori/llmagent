## Background
Commit `df6cf9eb` removed the duplicate Maintenance Rules section from `00_governance_04_documentation-checks.md` and replaced it with a cross-reference to `00_governance_01_documentation-policy.md#maintenance-rules`. However, the policy document still contains the raw three-bullet text without acknowledging the checks document as a consumer. This creates an asymmetry: one document defers to the other, but the source does not acknowledge the deference relationship.

## Problem
The deduplication effort fixed the symptom in the checks document but did not establish bidirectional awareness. The policy document's Maintenance Rules section has no pointer back to the checks document, leaving the deduplication one-directional and signaling incomplete cleanup.

## Reason for change
Completing the deduplication pattern established by commit df6cf9eb. Governance documents should have explicit, bidirectional awareness of their cross-reference relationships to prevent accidental divergence during future edits.

## Implementation intent
Add a single "Consumed by" cross-reference line after the three Maintenance Rules bullets in the policy document, pointing to the checks document's cross-reference. Keep the change minimal — one sentence addition, no restructuring.
