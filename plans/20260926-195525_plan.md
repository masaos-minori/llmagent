## Background
GV-007 refers to "Duplicate Related Link prohibition." In the Matrix, Tool/Review shows `check_docs_structure.py` and Status is `Existing`, indicating the feature is already implemented. However, the Follow-up Work Needed section lists item 4 as `GV-007: Implement Duplicate Related Link prohibition check`, creating a contradiction.

## Problem
The status of GV-007 is inconsistent between the Matrix and the Follow-up list, which may confuse implementers about whether the task is completed or not.

## Reason for change
