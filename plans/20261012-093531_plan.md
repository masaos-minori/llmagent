## Goal
- Close or explicitly leave open the assumption that no external consumer depends on the events-level meaning of `delivery_failure_count` and `cycle_failure_count`, and make the DLQ listing documentation state what that field currently holds.

## Priority
Low

## Scope
- **In-Scope**:
  - Obtain and record a dated owner answer on external use of the NACK response, the DLQ listing, and `eventbus.db.NackResult` counters, or record that no answer was obtained and why.
  - Record the outcome as the resolution of UNK-02 in `plans/done/20261012-084200_plan.md`.
  - State in the DLQ endpoint reference what the listing's `delivery_failure_count` holds under the per-consumer model.
- **Out-of-Scope**:
  - Any code, test, schema, or response-shape change.
  - Dropping or migrating the events-level columns; DLQ promotion and ACK behavior.
  - Other eventbus documents (see Risks for a drift found in `eventbus_03_dlq_operations.md`, which needs its own issue).

## Background
- `plans/done/20261012-084200_plan.md` kept the legacy-value contract for the `NackResult` counters and left UNK-02 open because external use cannot be established from the repository.
- Repository evidence (`Explicit in code`): no module outside `scripts/eventbus/` calls the NACK endpoint or `nack_event`; the only non-test reader of `NackResult` is the NACK route.
- The NACK response field `delivery_failure_count` carries the per-consumer count; `eventbus_12_ack_nack_endpoints.md` already says so.
- The DLQ listing (`fetch_dlq`) selects the events-level `delivery_failure_count` column, which the NACK path no longer writes (`Derived from confirmed evidence`: no `UPDATE events` statement changes it); `eventbus_04_dlq_endpoint.md` lists the field without saying what it holds.

## Problem
- An external client reading the DLQ listing or the NACK response may expect an event-level lifetime count and receive a legacy or per-consumer value; this cannot be ruled out from the repository, so the contract decision rests on an unverified assumption.
- The DLQ endpoint reference does not tell a reader what the listing's `delivery_failure_count` means.

## Reason for change
- Closing an open assumption behind a contract decision and removing a documentation ambiguity; no known defect, low urgency.

## Implementation intent
- Document current behavior from code evidence; ask the owner for the external-use fact rather than inferring it. If no answer is obtained, leave UNK-02 open with the reason.

## Requirements
- `REQ-001`: A dated owner answer on external use of the counters is recorded in the UNK-02 entry of `plans/done/20261012-084200_plan.md`, or the entry states that no answer was obtained and why.
- `REQ-002`: The DLQ endpoint reference states that the listing's `delivery_failure_count` is the stored events-level value, a legacy value that is not modified by NACK under the per-consumer model, and is not the per-consumer count.
- `REQ-003`: The statements in `eventbus_04_dlq_endpoint.md` and `eventbus_12_ack_nack_endpoints.md` about the meaning of `delivery_failure_count` agree with each other and with the recorded answer.
