## Background
While verifying `requires/done/20260818-222741_require.md` and building
`plans/20260819-181042_plan.md` (Standardize Terminology and Repair Verified
Markdown Quality Defects in docs/), a structural scan found that
`docs/01_overview-files-03-scripts.md` contains 10 separate `## Related
Documents` sections. Each one has the same self-reference bullet
(`` - `01_overview-files-03-scripts.md` ``) repeated 4 times plus one distinct
bullet (`` - [01_overview.md](01_overview.md) ``) — confirmed via a
per-section bullet-multiset script.

This is a larger instance of the "duplicate `## Related Documents` bullet"
defect that plan `plans/20260819-181042_plan.md` fixes at the bullet level
(removing 30 duplicate lines across the 10 sections). However, spot-reading
the surrounding content (e.g. lines 60-152) shows that entire content
blocks — not just the bullet list — repeat near-identically: the same
Japanese responsibility tables (`### 変更時の注意点`, `### 実装詳細の参照先`,
`## Keywords`, and a repeated `# ファイル構成` H1) reappear multiple times
throughout the file's 528 lines. This looks like accidental copy-paste
duplication of whole sections, not merely duplicate cross-reference bullets.

## Problem
Fixing only the `## Related Documents` bullet duplication (as scoped by the
current plan) leaves the underlying whole-section duplication in the file.
The file may still be substantially larger and more repetitive than it
should be, degrading readability and navigation.

## Reason for change
Untangling which of the 10 near-duplicate content blocks (if any) carries
unique information, and consolidating them, is a larger content-restructuring
task than "remove a duplicate bullet line." Per
`skills/require-to-plan/workflow.md` Out-of-scope ("unrelated refactoring",
"broad formatting-only rewrites"), this should not be folded into the current
terminology/duplicate-bullet plan without its own scoping and review.

## Implementation intent
- Read `docs/01_overview-files-03-scripts.md` end-to-end.
- Diff the 10 repeated content blocks against each other to determine
  whether they are byte-identical, near-identical with drift, or
  intentionally distinct per-topic sections that merely share a bullet
  format.
- If they are accidental duplicates: consolidate into a single canonical
  section per topic, removing the redundant copies (not just their
  `## Related Documents` bullets).
- If they are intentional per-topic sections (e.g., one per subsystem
  described in the file) that happen to share boilerplate: no structural
  change needed beyond what `plans/20260819-181042_plan.md` already does;
  close this issue as "no further action."
