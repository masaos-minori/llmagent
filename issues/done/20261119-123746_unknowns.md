# Unresolved blocking unknown: English `normalized_content` shape (kept projection vs dropped)

## Summary
The English chunker currently stores a stopword-stripped derivative in `content` and writes `NULL` `normalized_content`, so neither field preserves the original English text. Restoring the invariant requires setting English `content` to the unfiltered original (mandated by ADR-009 INV-01) and choosing exactly one of two shapes for `normalized_content`: (A) the stopword-stripped projection, so FTS5 indexes it while `content` stays original, or (B) `NULL`, dropping filtering entirely. `UNK-01` in the Plan records that this choice must be decided by the owner before any code change.

## Evidence Missing
ADR-009's "Current Decision Approval (pending)" (line 279) has no approver and no date, so there is no recorded owner decision favoring either shape. No FTS5 search-quality measurement exists to show whether keeping the stopword projection measurably improves retrieval, which is the tradeoff between Shape A and Shape B. The Issue's own AI Implementation Instruction states "Confirm the owner's decision first."

## Resolution Path
1. Obtain an owner/approver decision on whether English stopword filtering is kept as a normalized projection (Shape A) or removed entirely (Shape B).
2. Record the decision in ADR-009 as an invariant or an explicit exception, with the tradeoff.
3. Resume the Plan (`plans/20261119-123746_ragcrv002_english-content-stopword-loss.md`) at Step 8 once the decision is recorded, to freeze `Implementation Target Files` and archive the Issue.
