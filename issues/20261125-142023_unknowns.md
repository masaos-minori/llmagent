# Unresolved blocking unknown: task_id contract of tool audit events conflicts with ADR-001 INV-11

## Summary
UNK-01 of the Plan `plans/20261125-142023_mcpai007_dedup-audit-observability.md` is unresolved. The source Issue requires `task_id` of `tool_approval` / `tool_exec` events to be populated from workflow state and `turn_id` from turn state. ADR-001 item 10 and INV-11 (a Blocking, test-gated invariant) define `task_id` of `tool_approval` as the turn id, and `TestAuditApprovalInv11` asserts it. The two cannot both hold.

## Evidence Missing
- `scripts/agent/tool_audit.py:141,277` set `task_id=ctx.turn.current_turn_id`; `docs/10_adr/adr_02_02_001_agent-workflow-engine-mandatory.md` (item 10, INV-11), `docs/10_adr/adr_01_index.md` (INV-11 row), and `docs/23_agent/` (agent_03_03, agent_06_02, agent_06_04) state the same.
- No repository evidence decides whether the owner wants to amend INV-11.

## Resolution Path
Owner chooses one option and the Plan is amended, then `issue-to-plan` Step 8 re-runs:
- Option B (recommended; the Plan is drafted on it): keep `task_id` = turn id, add `turn_id` and record the workflow task id in `task_linkage`. No ADR amendment; additive and backward compatible.
- Option A: change `task_id` to the workflow task id. Requires amending ADR-001 item 10 / INV-11, the `adr_01_index.md` row, the three `docs/23_agent/` statements, and `tests/agent/test_tool_audit.py::TestAuditApprovalInv11`, plus a decision on `turn_start` / `turn_end` (Plan UNK-02).

## Blocking Impact
- **Blocking for:** REQ-004 (and the `tool_audit.py`, `models.py`, and audit test target rows), hence the Plan freeze.
- **Not blocking for:** REQ-001, REQ-002, REQ-003 (`dedup_state` transport and audit), REQ-005, REQ-007, and REQ-009 as drafted; they hold under either option.
