Apply the scope/requirements/conditions/exceptions/effects equivalence test
(defined in this same target file's own `### Deduplication Rules`, first
bullet) before removing any content — do not consolidate on the source Issue's
assumption alone. Where a comparison passes, replace the non-canonical text
with a reference in the `### Canonical References` format already defined in
the target file; where it fails, leave the text unchanged and record why (per
`skills/python-design/SKILL.md` Core Design Rules: avoid

  other two locations: rejected — the Plan's Scope explicitly keeps `Canonical
  Ownership Model`'s own text unchanged (`AC-5`); the other two locations are
  the ones being reduced to references, not the canonical source.
