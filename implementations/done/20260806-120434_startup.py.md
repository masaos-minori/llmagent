## Goal
Add an inline em-dash justification to each of the five currently-unjustified suppression
comments in `scripts/agent/startup.py` (four `# noqa: BLE001` in `_check_services()`, one
`# noqa: PLC0415` in `_check_workflow_schema()`), matching the existing convention already
used at lines 139 and 407, per `rules/coding.md` §Suppression governance.

## Scope
- In scope: comment-text-only edits to the 5 target `# noqa` lines listed below.
- Out of scope: the already-compliant suppressions at lines 139 and 407; any exception type,
  control flow, log message, or other code; any other check in `_check_services()` or
  elsewhere in the file; any other file.

## Assumptions
- The em-dash convention (`# noqa: <CODE> — <reason>`) shown at lines 139 and 407 is the
  required house style; no other format is acceptable.
- A short, check-specific reason is sufficient; no ticket/issue cross-reference is required
  (matching the precedent at line 407).
- No CI job parses or asserts on the literal noqa comment text, so wording changes carry no
  automated-check risk (per plan Assumptions / UNK-02).

## Design decisions
- One justification per line, each specific to that check's own failure semantics (not one
  generic sentence reused five times), so the reasoning is defensible on inspection rather than
  boilerplate.
- Keep each justification on the same physical line as the `# noqa` comment (append text after
  `— `), consistent with the two existing compliant examples, rather than moving the reason to a
  separate comment line above.

## Alternatives considered
- Reworking `_check_services()` to narrow the `except Exception` clauses to specific exception
  types instead of justifying the broad catch: rejected — out of scope per the plan (behavior
  must not change) and would require behavioral analysis of what exceptions each awaited call
  can actually raise.
- Adding a project-wide test that enforces "every noqa has an em-dash justification": rejected —
  out of scope per the plan (filed as a non-blocking follow-up risk instead).
