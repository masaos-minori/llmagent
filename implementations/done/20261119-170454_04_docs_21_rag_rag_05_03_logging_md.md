## Assumptions
- The record is emitted by `scripts/rag/ingestion/chunk_splitter.py` `process_file` alongside the existing INFO `chunked N chunks from X` line (which is not removed).
- The record carries `url`, `source_type="file"`, `stage_name="chunk"`, `discard_reason="below_min_chunk"`, `discarded_count`, `discarded_chars`; the message carries the human-readable count/length and never document content.
