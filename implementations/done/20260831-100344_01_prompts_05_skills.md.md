## Goal

Eliminate the duplicated five-step workflow procedure in `prompts/05_skills.md` by
rewriting `### Step Responsibilities` to purpose-only summaries with
cross-references to `### Tasks`, which remains the sole procedural definition
(REQ-001, REQ-002: remove duplicated procedural content; add cross-references).

## Scope

In scope: rewriting each `### Step Responsibilities` bullet (Step 1 through Step
5) and adding a cross-reference from each bullet to its matching `#### Step N`
subsection under `### Tasks`. Out of scope: any change to `### Tasks`' actual
step behavior, ordering, or numbering; any change to any other section of
`prompts/05_skills.md`; any change to any file other than `prompts/05_skills.md`.

## Assumptions

- **Corrected during Step 3 adversarial verification (this cycle)**: the Plan's
  assumption that `### Tasks` is *already* the more complete,
  currently-self-sufficient procedural description does not hold for every
  step. Direct re-read of `prompts/05_skills.md` found three literal
  back-references from `Tasks` to `Step Responsibilities` (`grep -n "Step
  Responsibilities" prompts/05_skills.md` → lines 271 [heading], 347, 359,
  387): `#### Step 1` opens with "Apply Step Responsibilities' Step 1
  definition above.", `#### Step 2` opens with "Apply Step Responsibilities'
  Step 2 definition above...", and `#### Step 4` closes with "Per Step
  Responsibilities: validate only here...". For Steps 1 and 2, `Step
  Responsibilities`' current bullet holds constraints `Tasks`' own body never
  restates (Step 1: "do not decide canonical ownership and do not edit any
  file in this step"; Step 2: the exact destination-file/heading and
  source-location-replacement detail, "do not edit any file in this step", and
  "an approval given for a different task does not count"). Naively reducing
  `Step Responsibilities` to bare summaries without also editing `Tasks` would
  leave `Tasks`' Step 1/Step 2 pointing at a section that no longer has a
  "definition" to apply, and would silently drop those constraints — violating
  this document's own Completion criteria and the Plan's `AC-3`. Step 3 has no
  such back-reference but is independently duplicative in the same way (`Step
  Responsibilities`' Step 3 bullet has an extra "Blocked: plan mismatch"
  escalation clause `Tasks`' Step 3 body lacks). Step 4's back-reference is
  safe to drop as a bare attribution edit, since `Tasks`' Step 4 body already
  states the same constraint in full inline ("do not redo Step 1's
  inventory or Step 2's planning or Step 3's edits") independent of `Step
  Responsibilities`. See the corrected Procedure below.
- `prompts/05_skills.md`'s own house style for intra-file pointers is a bare
  "above"/"below" reference (e.g. "Apply Editing Constraints above", "Report
  per Context Efficiency's format below"), not the formal `See <file path>,
  section "<heading>".` `### Canonical References` format — confirmed by
  reading the file's existing usage throughout `### Tasks` and elsewhere; that
  formal format is used for content actually relocated to a different file by
  a Relocation Plan, not for the file's own internal prose. The cross-references
  this document introduces follow the confirmed house style instead of the
  Plan's originally-specified `See prompts/05_skills.md, section "...".`
  format (see corrected Procedure step 4 below).
- No file outside `prompts/05_skills.md` references `Step Responsibilities`'
  specific procedural wording (confirmed via `rg -l "Step Responsibilities"
  --glob '!prompts/05_skills.md'` — no result other than this Plan's own
  now-archived source issue/plan documents).

## Design decisions

Keep `### Tasks` as the single canonical procedure; reduce `### Step
Responsibilities` to a short index of *why* each step exists, cross-referencing
`### Tasks` for *how* — mirroring the same canonical-source-plus-cross-reference
pattern `prompts/05_skills.md` already prescribes in its own `### Deduplication
Rules` section, applied reflexively to the file's own text (per
`skills/python-design/SKILL.md` Core Design Rules: avoid
implementation-reference duplication).

## Alternatives considered

- **Delete `Step Responsibilities` entirely** instead of rewriting it: rejected
  — the closing "Do not merge or skip any of these five steps..." constraint
  (lines 295-298) is not duplicated elsewhere and must be preserved; a bare
  index-with-references section still has value for a reader who wants the
  "why" without reading all of `Tasks`.
- **Merge `Step Responsibilities`'s content into `Tasks`** instead of keeping
  two sections: rejected — this would enlarge `Tasks` with prose the Plan's own
  Reason for Change identifies as the very duplication to remove, and would
  lose the "responsibility summary vs. procedure" separation the source Issue
  asked for.

## Implementation

### Procedure
1. Re-read the current text of `### Step Responsibilities` (lines 271-298) and
   `### Tasks` `#### Step 0`-`#### Step 5` (lines 331-400) in full.
2. For each of Step 1 through Step 5, compare the `Step Responsibilities`
   bullet's sentences against the matching `#### Step N` subsection sentence by
   sentence; mark each `Step Responsibilities` sentence as duplicated
   (equivalent content exists under `Tasks`), absorbed-by-reference (`Tasks`
   only has it via "Apply Step Responsibilities' Step N definition above" and
   does not otherwise state it), or unique-and-unreferenced (neither applies).
3. For Step 1 and Step 2 (found to be "absorbed-by-reference" per the
   corrected Assumptions above): inline the missing constraint text directly
   into `Tasks`' own `#### Step 1` / `#### Step 2` body, and delete the
   now-unnecessary "Apply Step Responsibilities' Step N definition above."
   opening sentence from each, so `Tasks` becomes self-contained without it.
4. For Step 3 (independently duplicative, no back-reference): move its unique
   "Blocked: plan mismatch" escalation clause into `Tasks`' `#### Step 3` body
   (which currently only names the `Blocked: plan mismatch` outcome without
   the escalation instruction).
5. For Step 4: remove only the "Per Step Responsibilities:" attribution prefix
   from `Tasks`' `#### Step 4` closing sentence, keeping the constraint text
   itself (`Tasks` already states it in full, independent of `Step
   Responsibilities`).
6. Rewrite each `Step Responsibilities` bullet (Step 1 through Step 5) to a
   short purpose/responsibility summary, now that step 3-5 above ensure
   `Tasks` holds every constraint in full.
7. Append a cross-reference to each rewritten bullet using the file's existing
   bare "above"/"below" house style (e.g. "See Step N under Tasks below."),
   not the formal `### Canonical References` `See <file path>, section
   "...".` format — that format is reserved for content relocated to a
   *different* file, not for this file's own internal prose (see corrected
   Assumptions above).
8. Leave the closing "Do not merge or skip any of these five steps..."
   paragraph (currently lines 295-298) unchanged.

### Method
Direct text edit (Markdown), applied to both `### Step Responsibilities` and
(for steps 1-3 only, per the correction above) the affected `#### Step N`
bodies under `### Tasks`. No code generation, no automated transformation
tool — the comparison in step 2 above must be done by reading both sections,
not by a mechanical diff, since wording differs between the two sections even
where meaning is equivalent.

- Do not touch `### Tasks`' heading text, step numbering, `#### Step 0`, or
  `#### Step 5` — those are unaffected by this correction.
- Do not touch `#### Step 4`'s five-bullet "Additionally confirm..." list —

- After editing, the five bullets under `Step Responsibilities` should each be
  noticeably shorter than before, and each should end with one bare
