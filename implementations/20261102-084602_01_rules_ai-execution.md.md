### Procedure

### Method
- Bullet 1 (announcement, `REQ-001`): before a command expected to take longer than one minute, and before any command started in the background, emit exactly one line stating what is awaited and the expected duration. The line may be the tool call description or a separate line. A command expected to finish within one minute gets no announcement, so ordinary short steps add no message. The threshold is the default and a workflow does not override it.
- Bullet 2 (no polling, `REQ-002`): do not add polling or periodic messages while the wait lasts. When the platform delivers a background command's completion, report its result in one line (exit status and the outcome that matters), and omit nothing that Command Results requires.
- Keep each bullet to one or two sentences, and do not restate the Output section's rules.

### Details
- Anchor the edit on the unique text "Report progress once per step, in one line, after the step completes." in the section starting at the heading `## Progress Reporting (Base)`.
- The new bullets must not alter the existing exemption: a step that completes as expected still omits its report; the announcement is a pre-wait line, and the result line replaces, not adds to, the step's own report when the step is the background command.
