## Goal

Eliminate the duplicated five-step workflow procedure in `prompts/05_skills.md` by
rewriting `### Step Responsibilities` to purpose-only summaries with
cross-references to `### Tasks`, which remains the sole procedural definition
(REQ-001, REQ-002: remove duplicated procedural content; add cross-references).

## Scope

In scope: rewriting each `### Step Responsibilities` bullet (Step 1 through Step
5) and adding a cross-reference from each bullet to its matching `#### Step N`
subsection under `### Tasks`. Out of scope: any change to `### Tasks`' actual
step behavior, ordering, or numbering; any change to any other section of
`prompts/05_skills.md`; any change to any file other than `prompts/05_skills.md`.

## Assumptions

- `### Tasks`' `#### Step 0` through `#### Step 5` subsections remain the more
  complete and currently-authoritative procedural description, confirmed
  unchanged since the Plan was written (re-verified via `grep -n` immediately
  before writing this document: `### Step Responsibilities` still at lines
  271-298, `### Tasks` still at lines 331-400).
- No file outside `prompts/05_skills.md` references `Step Responsibilities`'
  specific procedural wording (confirmed via `rg -l "Step Responsibilities"
  --glob '!prompts/05_skills.md'` — no result other than this Plan's own
  now-archived source issue/plan documents).

## Design decisions

Keep `### Tasks` as the single canonical procedure; reduce `### Step
Responsibilities` to a short index of *why* each step exists, cross-referencing
`### Tasks` for *how* — mirroring the same canonical-source-plus-cross-reference
pattern `prompts/05_skills.md` already prescribes in its own `### Deduplication
Rules` section, applied reflexively to the file's own text (per
`skills/python-design/SKILL.md` Core Design Rules: avoid
implementation-reference duplication).

## Alternatives considered

- **Delete `Step Responsibilities` entirely** instead of rewriting it: rejected
  — the closing "Do not merge or skip any of these five steps..." constraint
  (lines 295-298) is not duplicated elsewhere and must be preserved; a bare
  index-with-references section still has value for a reader who wants the
  "why" without reading all of `Tasks`.
- **Merge `Step Responsibilities`'s content into `Tasks`** instead of keeping
  two sections: rejected — this would enlarge `Tasks` with prose the Plan's own
  Reason for Change identifies as the very duplication to remove, and would
  lose the "responsibility summary vs. procedure" separation the source Issue
  asked for.

## Implementation

### Target file
`prompts/05_skills.md`

### Procedure
1. Re-read the current text of `### Step Responsibilities` (lines 271-298) and
   `### Tasks` `#### Step 0`-`#### Step 5` (lines 331-400) in full.
2. For each of Step 1 through Step 5, compare the `Step Responsibilities`
   bullet's sentences against the matching `#### Step N` subsection sentence by
   sentence; mark each `Step Responsibilities` sentence as duplicated
   (equivalent content exists under `Tasks`) or unique (no equivalent exists).
3. Rewrite each `Step Responsibilities` bullet to keep only its unique
   sentences plus a one-clause statement of the step's purpose, dropping every
   sentence marked duplicated in step 2.
4. Append a cross-reference to each rewritten bullet, in the format `See
   prompts/05_skills.md, section "Step N: <heading text>".` (per this file's own
   `### Canonical References` section), pointing at the matching `#### Step N`
   subsection.
5. Leave the closing "Do not merge or skip any of these five steps..."
   paragraph (currently lines 295-298) unchanged.

### Method
Direct text edit (Markdown), applied section-by-section within
`### Step Responsibilities` only. No code generation, no automated
transformation tool — the comparison in step 2 above must be done by reading
both sections, not by a mechanical diff, since wording differs between the two
sections even where meaning is equivalent.

### Details
- Do not touch `### Tasks`' heading text or step numbering; the cross-references
  introduced in Procedure step 4 must match the exact current heading text
  (e.g. `#### Step 2: Relocation Plan`) so the reference resolves correctly.
- If Procedure step 2 finds a `Step Responsibilities` sentence with no
  equivalent in `Tasks`, keep it verbatim in the rewritten bullet rather than
  compressing or paraphrasing it — this Plan's `AC-3` requires no requirement,
  prohibition, condition, or exception to be lost.
- After editing, the five bullets under `Step Responsibilities` should each be
  noticeably shorter than before, and each should end with one `See
  prompts/05_skills.md, section "..."` reference sentence.
