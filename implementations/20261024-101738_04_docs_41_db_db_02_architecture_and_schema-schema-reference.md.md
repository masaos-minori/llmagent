### Method

`tasks.idempotency_key` is a globally unique identifier for its task row, generated by
`create_task()` in `agent/workflow/task_ops.py`. It is not derived from `session_id` or
`turn_number`; the UNIQUE constraint is a backstop against a future derivation regression, not the
mechanism that gives a row its identity. `turn_number` is informational, process-local metadata, and
resume identity is `task_id`. A query for the tasks of a session and turn uses the recorded
`session_id` and `turn_number` columns (`get_task_by_session()`), not the key. Rows written before
this contract keep their values and remain readable.
